THE FIELD GUIDE

Keep the fire burning.

Clear each castle, then hold Interact at its banner for five seconds. Rescue all nine survivor groups after clearing nearby enemies. Hold all nine castles to break the plague nest's protection, then destroy it.

Fight & explore

WASD to move. Click the world to capture your mouse; move it to look. Left mouse attacks, right mouse blocks. E interacts, H heals, Q performs a wide Sword Sweep. V switches views. B opens castle management. Gold beacons mark survivor camps; follow the compass objective to find them. Esc releases your mouse. Eight weapon classes — longsword, bastard sword, warhammer, poleaxe, zweihander, rapier, halberd and dane axe — each have their own reach, damage, stamina and recovery.

Xbox controller

Press a controller button to connect. Left stick moves, right stick looks. RT / RB attacks, LT blocks, A interacts, X heals, B performs Sword Sweep, Y switches camera. D-pad ↑ Follow, ↓ Hold, → Attack, ← recruit Guard. LB cycles unlocked weapons. Menu opens this guide and the Armory. Use the D-pad and A in menus.

Build your fellowship

Rescue groups for four recruits each. At a friendly banner, spend 3 iron and one recruit on a companion or guard. Follow, Hold and Attack command your companions. Guards stay at their castle.

Stamina, noise & armor

Weapon stamina cost, reach, damage and recovery are shown in the Armory. Blocking drains 22 per second. Sword Sweep costs 40 stamina with a 7-second cooldown. Stop attacking and lower your shield to recover. Heavy armor reduces damage by 30%, but slows movement and recovery. Change armor freely at a friendly castle. Sword clashes and gate attacks attract undead through the fog. Recovery windows prevent new siege waves, but existing enemies remain dangerous.

Regions & castle artillery

Capture all three castles in a region to earn a one-time 40 wood and 20 iron bounty. Hold the region for 24 wood, 12 iron and 2 healing supplies each minute; its artillery reloads faster. Every captured castle’s catapult fires automatically at nearby undead. Upgrades increase damage. Travel between friendly banners through Camp management outside sieges and nearby combat.

Dismemberment

Sword strikes can sever a weakened skeleton’s arm, reducing its damage. Finishing sword blows remove its head. These effects apply to skeleton enemies, not armored knights.

Defend your kingdom

Repair gates for 8 wood + 2 iron. Build a barricade for 12 wood + 4 iron. Undead must break the barricade, then the gate, to enter. Friendly troops can pass through. Siege warnings give 30 seconds to prepare. If undead breach the gate and occupy an undefended banner for eight seconds, the castle falls.

Fallen soldiers return as corrupted knights after 7 seconds. You fall for 12 seconds: another player must hold Interact within 3 meters for 3 uninterrupted seconds to revive you. A following companion can help when nearby enemies are cleared. If rescue fails, your body becomes a 220-health corrupted knight and you respawn 8 seconds later. Losing every friendly castle after your first capture ends the campaign. Online games continue while this guide is open.

THE FELLOWSHIP ARMORY

Choose your weapon.

Recover weapons from blue-marked racks. A warhammer waits beside the starting camp; the castles hold more.

R / Xbox LB cycles unlocked weapons. I opens this armory. Each player equips independently. Switching takes 0.45 seconds and cannot cancel an attack. Two-handed weapons parry 45% of frontal damage; shields block 80%. The campaign continues while this menu is open.

Camp management

Enter a friendly castle or approach its entrance to build. Bastion costs 24 wood + 10 iron; Citadel costs 40 wood + 18 iron. Each tier adds 120 gate health and 60 barricade health, restores defenses, and heals nearby allies. The campaign continues while the guide is open.
