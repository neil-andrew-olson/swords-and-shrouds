THE FIELD GUIDE

Keep the fire burning.

Clear each castle, then hold Interact at its banner for five seconds. Rescue all three survivor groups after clearing nearby enemies. Hold all three castles to break the plague nest's protection, then destroy it.

Fight & explore

WASD to move. Click the world to capture your mouse; move it to look. Left mouse attacks, right mouse blocks. E interacts, H heals. V switches views. B opens castle management. Gold beacons mark survivor camps; follow the compass objective to find them. Esc releases your mouse.

Xbox controller

Press a controller button to connect. Left stick moves, right stick looks. RT / RB attacks, LT blocks, A interacts, X heals, Y switches camera. D-pad ↑ Follow, ↓ Hold, → Attack, ← recruit Guard. Menu opens this guide. Use the D-pad and A in menus.

Build your fellowship

Rescue groups for four recruits each. At a friendly banner, spend 3 iron and one recruit on a companion or guard. Follow, Hold and Attack command your companions. Guards stay at their castle.

Defend your kingdom

Repair gates for 8 wood + 2 iron. Build a barricade for 12 wood + 4 iron. Siege warnings give 30 seconds to prepare. If undead breach the gate and occupy an undefended banner for eight seconds, the castle falls.

Fallen soldiers return as corrupted knights after 7 seconds. You respawn after 8 seconds. Losing every friendly castle after your first capture ends the campaign. Online games continue while this guide is open.

Camp management

Enter a friendly castle or approach its entrance to build. Bastion costs 24 wood + 10 iron; Citadel costs 40 wood + 18 iron. Each tier adds 120 gate health and 60 barricade health, restores defenses, and heals nearby allies. The campaign continues while the guide is open.
